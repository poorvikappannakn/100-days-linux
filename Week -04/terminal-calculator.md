# Terminal Calculator

## expr

Used for performing arithmetic operations in Linux.

### Syntax

expr number operator number

### Examples

expr 5 + 3

expr 2025 - 2024

expr 24 * 7

expr 60 / 5

### Learning

* Addition
* Subtraction
* Multiplication
* Division
* Importance of spaces between operands and operators

### Note

Spaces are mandatory.

Correct:

expr 5 + 3

Incorrect:

expr 5+3
